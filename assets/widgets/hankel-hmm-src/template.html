How to read the spectrum. Population rank and sample rank are different claims. Exact probabilities stop at the process rank; a finite-sample Hankel block is generically higher rank, often full rank, with a small noise tail below the signal.
About “Hankel error.” The fourth curve is the relative Frobenius residual of this finite block. The spectral-norm residual is instead σr+1. Neither should be silently conflated with KL or word-probability error.
